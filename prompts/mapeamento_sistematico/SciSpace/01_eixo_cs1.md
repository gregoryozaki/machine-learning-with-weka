Conduct a Systematic Literature Review (SLR) on the classification of environmental waste levels in datacenter racks, with special focus on AI training datacenters and high-performance computing infrastructure used for Artificial Intelligence workloads.

The review should investigate how the literature characterizes environmental impact,energy waste, operational inefficiencies, and sustainability issues in datacenters, servers, and rack-level infrastructure.

The SLR must include:
* A clear description of environmental waste and sustainability problems caused by datacenters used for AI training and large-scale computational processing;

* Studies related to energy efficiency, cooling systems, carbon emissions, water consumption, thermal behavior, and environmental impact in datacenter infrastructure;

* Operational, thermal, energetic, computational, and environmental factors associated with inefficient resource usage and environmental waste in datacenter racks;

* Metrics used to evaluate environmental efficiency, including energy consumption, cooling efficiency, temperature variation, carbon footprint, PUE (Power Usage Effectiveness), and water usage;

* Classification techniques, Machine Learning methods, and Artificial Intelligence algorithms used to analyze, classify, predict, detect, or optimize environmental inefficiencies in datacenters;

* Studies that use the Weka tool or mention experiments performed with Weka for classification, prediction, anomaly detection, or environmental data analysis;

* The most common classification algorithms used in the literature, such as Decision Trees, Random Forest, Naive Bayes, SVM, KNN, Logistic Regression, Neural Networks, and other Machine Learning approaches;

* Existing datasets, simulations, monitoring systems, and experimental methodologies used in environmental analysis of datacenters;

* The main technical and operational challenges related to environmental impact classification in AI datacenters;

* Current limitations and research gaps regarding the classification of environmental waste levels in datacenter racks.

Include only peer-reviewed scientific papers, conference articles, and academic publications directly related to datacenters, AI infrastructure, sustainable computing, green IT, Machine Learning, environmental monitoring, and infrastructure optimization.

Prioritize:
* Articles that use Weka;
* Studies involving AI training datacenters;
* Studies focused on environmental impact classification;
* Research applying Machine Learning classification algorithms to datacenter sustainability problems.

Exclude:
* Studies unrelated to datacenters, servers, or rack infrastructure;
* Studies focused only on computational performance without environmental analysis;
* Commercial materials, blogs, or non-scientific documents;
* Duplicated studies;
* Studies without abstracts or without sufficient methodological information.
